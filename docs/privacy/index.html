SCYTALE

Privacy Policy · Effective 21 November 2026

Summary

Scytale collects no data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no network connections. Everything you put into Scytale stays on your iPhone, encrypted.

What stays on your device

  • Files, photos and messages you add are encrypted on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM. Decrypted content is shown in memory only and is never written to storage.
  • The keys that protect your vault are kept on your iPhone: one in the Secure Enclave, released only by Face ID, and others in the iOS Keychain set to "this device only".
  • The vault is excluded from iCloud Backup and from computer backups, and is never synced to iCloud.
  • An on-device audit log records security events (for example logins and lockouts) with their time. It never records file names or content.

Permissions Scytale asks for

  • Camera: to take photos that go straight into the encrypted vault, never into Photos.
  • Face ID: to release the vault key from the Secure Enclave. Face data is handled by iOS and is never accessible to Scytale.
  • Photos: only when you ask Scytale to delete the originals of photos it has encrypted.
  • Notifications: local notifications about logins, created on your iPhone. No push service is used.

Messages and files you send to another device

Scytale's Courier lets you seal a message, or a file from your vault (not photos), for another device you have paired with. Scytale only creates the encrypted file or text; you choose how to send it (for example by email), and Scytale does not transmit it. The service you use to send it only ever sees encrypted data and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Information Apple may share

  • If you have chosen in iOS settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer. Scytale itself sends nothing.
  • During beta testing through TestFlight, Apple shares the name and email address you use for TestFlight, and any feedback you submit, with the developer, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Deleting your data

All Scytale data is on your iPhone. Delete the app to remove it, or use Panic wipe inside Scytale to destroy the vault, its keys and its log immediately. There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

Children

Scytale is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
